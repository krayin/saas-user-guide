Billing & Invoices ​

This page is for a tenant — a business managing its own subscription and invoices.

Everything about what you pay lives under Subscription in your workspace: your current plan, the option to change it, and every invoice you've been charged.

What you'll learn

  • Where to find your plan and billing
  • How to change or cancel your subscription
  • How to view and download your invoices

Your plan ​

Open Subscription → Plans in your workspace.

Your active plan is marked Current Plan, with its price and the validity dates of the current cycle — when it started and when it renews. The other plans show what you'd move to.

  • Upgrade — switch to a larger plan for more capacity.
  • Downgrade — switch to a smaller one.
  • Cancel Subscription — stop your subscription. You'll be asked for a reason to confirm.

WARNING

Cancelling ends your paid subscription. Your CRM stays reachable until the current cycle you've already paid for runs out, after which you'll need to choose a plan again to keep using it.

Your invoices ​

Open Subscription → Invoices.

Every charge is listed with its Increment Id (invoice number), Amount, Payment Method, Status and date. You see only your own invoices — no one else's.

The download icon in the Actions column saves the invoice as a PDF, ready for your own bookkeeping.

TIP

These are the same invoices your platform operator sees on their side. If you're missing one, they can re-send it to you — just ask.
