# Choosing a Plan

*This page is for a **tenant** — a business deciding which subscription to be on.*

Your plan sets your price and your limits — how many team members, leads and contacts you can
have. This page explains how to read the plans and how to switch between them.

::: info What you'll learn
- How to read the pricing page
- What monthly vs. yearly means for you
- How to upgrade, downgrade or cancel later
:::

## Reading the plans

<ImagePopup src="/images/choosing-a-plan/pricing-plans.png" alt="The pricing page" />

Each plan card shows its **price** and what you get — typically **Team seats**, **Leads**,
**Contacts** and **Workflows**, with **Unlimited** where a plan lifts the cap. One plan may be
marked **Recommended**.

- **Monthly / Yearly** — use the toggle at the top to switch the prices between billing every
  month and billing once a year. Yearly usually costs less overall, and the toggle shows how
  much you save.
- A plan showing a struck-through price has an **offer** — you pay the lower figure.
- The **Free Trial** card, if shown, lets you try the product for a set number of days with no
  card required. See [Free Trials](/subscriptions/trials).

Select **Select** on the plan you want to start signing up, or **Start Free Trial** to begin a
trial. New here? That leads into [Registration](/tenants/registration).

## Changing your plan later

You're not locked in. Inside your workspace, open **Subscription → Plans** to see every plan
with your current one marked.

<ImagePopup src="/images/billing-and-invoices/tenant-billing.png" alt="Managing your plan from inside the workspace" />

- **Upgrade** — move to a larger plan. Use this when you're bumping against your limits.
- **Downgrade** — move to a smaller plan.
- **Cancel Subscription** — end your current subscription (you'll be asked for a reason).

::: tip
Limits shown as **Unlimited** (internally `-1`) mean there's no cap on that resource. A plan
with a number — *Leads: 5000* — caps you at that figure, after which you're prompted to move
up.
:::
