# Billing & Invoices

*This page is for a **tenant** — a business managing its own subscription and invoices.*

Everything about what you pay lives under **Subscription** in your workspace: your current
plan, the option to change it, and every invoice you've been charged.

::: info What you'll learn
- Where to find your plan and billing
- How to change or cancel your subscription
- How to view and download your invoices
:::

## Your plan

Open **Subscription → Plans** in your workspace.

<ImagePopup src="/images/billing-and-invoices/tenant-billing.png" alt="Your current plan and options" />

Your active plan is marked **Current Plan**, with its price and the **validity** dates of the
current cycle — when it started and when it renews. The other plans show what you'd move to.

- **Upgrade** — switch to a larger plan for more capacity.
- **Downgrade** — switch to a smaller one.
- **Cancel Subscription** — stop your subscription. You'll be asked for a reason to confirm.

::: warning
Cancelling ends your paid subscription. Your CRM stays reachable until the current cycle you've
already paid for runs out, after which you'll need to choose a plan again to keep using it.
:::

## Your invoices

Open **Subscription → Invoices**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/billing-and-invoices/tenant-invoices.png" alt="Your invoices" />

Every charge is listed with its **Increment Id** (invoice number), **Amount**, **Payment
Method**, **Status** and date. You see only your own invoices — no one else's.

The download icon in the **Actions** column saves the invoice as a **PDF**, ready for your
own bookkeeping.

::: tip
These are the same invoices your platform operator sees on their side. If you're missing one,
they can re-send it to you — just ask.
:::
