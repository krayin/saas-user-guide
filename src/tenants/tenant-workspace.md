# The Tenant Workspace

*This page is for a **tenant** — a business using the CRM it signed up for.*

Once you've registered, you have a complete Krayin CRM of your own. This page is a quick tour
of what's inside and how it's yours alone.

::: info What you'll learn
- Where to sign in and what you'll see
- What's in your workspace
- How your data stays private to you
:::

## Signing in

Your CRM lives at your own address — `yourusername.yourplatform.com` — and you sign in at
`/admin/login` there with the email and password you registered with.

<ImagePopup src="/images/tenant-workspace/tenant-dashboard.png" alt="A tenant's CRM dashboard" />

The dashboard greets you with the numbers that matter — lead value, total leads, quotations,
contacts and organizations, your open leads by stage, and revenue charts — all drawn from
*your* data.

## What's inside

Your workspace is a full CRM. From the sidebar you can reach:

- **Leads** — your sales opportunities, moving through pipeline stages.
- **Quotes** — quotations you send to your contacts.
- **Contacts** and **Organizations** — the people and companies you work with.
- **Mail**, **Activities** and **Products** — the tools around your day-to-day selling.
- **Settings** — your own users, roles, pipelines and preferences.
- **Subscription** — your plan and invoices (see
  [Billing & Invoices](/tenants/billing-and-invoices)).

It's an ordinary Krayin CRM — everything Krayin does, you have.

## It's entirely yours

Everything in your workspace belongs to you and only you. You can't see another business's
leads or contacts, and no other tenant can see yours — the platform keeps every tenant's data
completely separate. You manage your own team, your own pipelines and your own settings,
independently of everyone else on the platform.

::: tip
Your subscription decides your limits — how many team members, leads or contacts you can
create. When you approach a limit, you'll be prompted to upgrade. See
[Choosing a Plan](/tenants/choosing-a-plan).
:::
